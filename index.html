About EUROPT 2027

About EUROPT 2027

The 24st EUROPT Conference on Advances in Continuous Optimization (EUROPT 2027) focuses on recent advances in continuous optimization, covering theory, algorithms, software, and applications. We aim to bring together both theoretical and applied researchers in the field. The event features invited plenary lectures, a tribute to Dimitri P. Bertsekas, as well as invited and contributed sessions. The conference is preceded by the 7th EUROPT Summer School, taking place on July 19–20.

Travel

Reaching Athens

The nearest major airport to Athens is Athens International Airport “Eleftherios Venizelos” (ATH), located about 35 km east of the city center. Direct trains on Metro Line 3 take travelers to Syntagma Square in around 40 minutes. Tickets can be bought (for approximately €9) at vending machines inside the terminal, before entering the platform.

Travelers who avoid flying may reach Athens with an adventurous combination of train, bus and ferry, and the best route depends on where they start. From the Balkans or Turkey, the usual route goes through Thessaloniki, Greece's second city. From Thessaloniki, modern InterCity trains reach Athens in about 4 hours, and tickets can be bought online or at the station for roughly €30 to €50. From western and central Europe, a popular alternative is to take a train to Bari, Ancona or Venice in Italy, then an overnight ferry to Patras in Greece. From Patras, a train and bus connection continues to Athens. Interrail and Eurail pass holders may get discounted or free ferry tickets on some routes.
